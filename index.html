DATA & METHODOLOGY

Our research draws on data from FracTracker Alliance, the U.S. Census Bureau, EIA, FCC, FEMA, and additional public sources. The county map uses our cleaned county-level dataset; water availability and stress are watershed-level USGS annual values summarized to counties, with coverage varying by measure.
